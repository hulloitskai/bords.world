## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Bords World is a small interactive bird collection. The first release centers on browsing illustrated bird cards.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Visitors browse a fanned deck with a mouse wheel or trackpad on desktop and a finger swipe on mobile. The selected card stands out at the center, and its name updates as it takes the front position. The deck wraps and starts with the twelve illustrated birds. Visitors can play recordings for the selected bird on demand.

The app uses React, Vite, and shadcn with a GitHub Pages build. The bird catalog links display names, card artwork, and recordings.
