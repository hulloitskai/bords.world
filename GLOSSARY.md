# Bords World

An illustrated collection of birds.

## Language

**Bird Card**:
An illustrated playing card that represents a bird.

**Deck**:
The ordered collection of Bird Cards visitors can browse.

**Selected Card**:
The Bird Card at the center of the Deck and the focus of the current view.

**Recording**:
A playable example of a Bird Card’s song, call, or bill rattle. A Bird Card can have several recordings of the same kind.
